Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ policy_updated_date if policy_updated_date else '2026' }}

This policy is written in plain language so parents and kids can actually read it. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer — if you're relying on this for a real launch, have it checked against South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) before you rely on it.

What we collect

  • Account info: your username, email address, and password (we only ever store a scrambled/hashed version of your password — we can't see it).
  • If you're under 18: a parent or guardian's email address, so they can be told about the account.
  • What you build: the code you write and save in the Playground, your lesson progress, quiz scores, XP, badges, and streaks.
  • Questions you ask the AI tutor: the text of your questions and the lesson you're on, so the tutor can give a relevant answer.
  • Basic technical info: your IP address, used only to prevent spam signups and abuse of the AI tutor.

We do not collect your real name, address, phone number, or any payment information — Nexa Pals is free and we don't ask for any of that.

How we use it

  • To run your account, save your progress, and show you the leaderboard and badges.
  • To send your question to an AI provider (see "Third parties" below) and show you the reply.
  • To stop bots and abuse (rate limits on signups and AI requests).
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising — Nexa Pals has no ads.

Third parties

When you ask the AI tutor a question, or use "Explain this code" or "Generate a project," your question and the relevant lesson context are sent to our AI provider, Groq, to generate a reply. Groq does not retain the content of these requests for training. Nexa Pals does not share your username, email, or any other account details with them — only the text of the question itself. We do not use Google Gemini for accounts belonging to anyone under 18, in line with Gemini's own terms of service.

If you use the contact form, your message is sent to our support inbox via email (Gmail SMTP) so we can reply.

Children's privacy & parental consent

Nexa Pals is built for learners aged 10 and up, including children under 18. If you tell us you're under 18 when you sign up, we ask for a parent or guardian's email address and send them a message so they know the account exists and can ask us to review or remove it at any time. AI features (the tutor, code helper, and project generator) stay switched off for that account until a parent verifies the email we sent — so an AI conversation with your child never starts without a parent knowing about it first.

A parent or guardian can email us at {{ contact_email if contact_email else 'info@nexapals.co.za' }} at any time to see what information we hold about their child, correct it, or have it deleted.

How long we keep your data

We keep your account and progress for as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete your account, we'll delete your personal information within a reasonable time, except where we need to keep something for a legal reason.

Your rights

You (or your parent/guardian, if you're under 18) can ask us at any time to:

  • See what information we have about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your account and personal information

Email {{ contact_email if contact_email else 'info@nexapals.co.za' }} or use the contact form for any of these.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you logged in. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Changes to this policy

If we make a meaningful change to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about privacy? Email {{ contact_email if contact_email else 'info@nexapals.co.za' }} or use our contact page.
