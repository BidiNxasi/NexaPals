Our Mission

Nexa Pals is a free coding education platform built for South African kids aged 10 and up. We teach HTML, CSS, and JavaScript through hands-on projects, arcade games, AI-powered tutoring, and a live code playground — no prior experience needed.

Our goal is simple: make coding feel like play, reward curiosity with XP and badges, and give every child — regardless of background — the digital skills that will define the next generation.

Built at UFS

Nexa Pals was designed and built by a student team at the University of the Free State (UFS), Faculty of Natural and Agricultural Sciences, Department of Computer Science and Informatics.

This project is part of our commitment to using technology to uplift communities and give young South Africans a head start in the digital economy.
